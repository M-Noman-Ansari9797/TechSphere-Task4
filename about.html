Our story

It started with one birthday cake for a cousin

Meethi Yaadein began in a home kitchen in Karachi, when our founder started baking birthday cakes for family because bakery cakes never tasted like the ones her mother made. Word spread through relatives, then neighbours, then their neighbours. Today, every cake we make still comes from that same kitchen, mixed by hand, in small batches, the day it's delivered. We never bake ahead and freeze — if it's not fresh, it doesn't leave our kitchen.
